Privacy policy

How DMV Prep handles your study, purchase and analytics information.

Effective: November 3, 2026 · Operator: MD Teknoloji Bilişim ve Ticaret Anonim Şirketi

Study information on your device

DMV Prep stores your selected state and language, study preferences, answers, progress and practice results locally. Study progress is not synchronized to the cloud. An optional Premium account does not upload your answers or study history. Removing the app may remove this local information. Aggregate study-session counts and outcomes may be included in the analytics described below.

Purchases and restoration

Premium is an optional, one-time purchase. Apple or Google processes the payment; DMV Prep does not receive your full payment-card information. RevenueCat processes purchase history, transaction and store information, a pseudonymous app-user identifier and technical information to validate purchases, provide Premium access, prevent fraud and restore purchases. Purchase information also supports purchase reporting.

Without an account, the app uses RevenueCat’s anonymous app-user identifiers. An account is not required for studying or purchasing. If you choose to sign in, RevenueCat receives a random DMV account identifier to link Premium access across devices; we do not send your email address to RevenueCat. Apple and Google maintain their own purchase records.

Optional Premium accounts

In versions that support accounts, you may register or sign in with an email and password through Google Firebase Authentication in Den’s shared authentication project. Firebase processes your email, password credentials, authentication identifiers and security-related connection information. Our Den backend stores a separate DMV account identifier, Firebase user identifier, email address and account timestamps. Passwords are handled by Firebase and are not stored by the DMV backend.

We use these records for sign-in, email verification, password recovery, connecting Premium and account deletion. DMV profiles and Premium entitlements are separate from Den application profiles and subscriptions. If an email already has a Den sign-in, it uses the same Firebase credentials; password changes apply to that shared sign-in. Account creation is optional.

Product analytics

DMV Prep uses Mixpanel to understand and improve how the app is used. Events may include screens viewed, selected study state and language, study-session counts and outcomes, purchase or restore outcomes, app version and OTA update number. Mixpanel also processes a pseudonymous app/device identifier and technical device information.

The app does not send your name, email address, question text or individual answers in analytics events. Automatic Mixpanel events and IP-based geolocation are disabled in the app. Network requests still reach the providers, which may process technical connection information. Selecting a study state is not a request for your device’s location.

App updates

DMV Prep can use Expo’s EAS Update service to check for and download compatible application updates. These requests involve app, platform, version and network-connection information. They do not upload your stored study answers or progress. See Expo’s privacy policy.

Purpose and sharing

We use this information to operate accounts and Premium, restore access, respond to support requests and understand product usage. DMV Prep contains no advertising, and its implementation does not use these events to track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

Service providers process information for these purposes under their own applicable terms and privacy notices: RevenueCat, Mixpanel, Apple and Google. Mixpanel uses its default U.S. service endpoint in this app.

Retention, choices and requests

Local study progress can be reset for the selected state in Settings. Removing the app can remove local progress, but does not automatically remove purchase or analytics records held by service providers. Purchase records are used to support access and restoration over time; provider-held information is also subject to provider retention settings and applicable legal obligations.

You may contact us with questions or requests about access, correction or deletion. The information we can locate depends on the records and identifiers we can reliably associate with you; the app does not collect a name or email address with its analytics. Requests are subject to applicable law and purchase-record requirements. Please provide your platform, app version and relevant transaction reference if needed. Do not send passwords, full payment-card details or identity documents.

Account deletion

To delete a DMV account, open Settings → Premium account → Delete DMV Prep account and confirm your password. This removes its DMV backend profile and RevenueCat customer connection. If the Firebase sign-in is not used by a Den app account, it is also deleted. Existing Den accounts and their data are preserved. A provider failure may delay completion while the request is retried.

Deleting an account does not refund a store purchase or erase local study progress. Store records and information that providers must retain for legal, security or transaction purposes may remain. For deletion without access to the app, follow our account and data deletion instructions. Account records are otherwise retained while the account is in use. Anonymous analytics are not automatically linked to an email.

Support messages

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, including your email address. We use it to respond to your request.

Audience and external sites

DMV Prep is a driver-knowledge study aid, not a service directed to children under 13. Links to official handbooks and external websites open in the app’s browser; those sites have their own practices. These support pages have no analytics or third-party scripts. They are hosted by GitHub Pages, which processes hosting-related technical information under GitHub’s privacy statement.
