Privacy policy

How DMV Prep handles your study, purchase and analytics information.

Effective: November 2, 2026 · Operator: MD Teknoloji Bilişim ve Ticaret Anonim Şirketi

Study information on your device

DMV Prep stores your selected state and language, study preferences, answers, progress and practice results locally. There is no DMV Prep account or cloud synchronization of study progress. Removing the app may remove this local information. Aggregate study-session counts and outcomes may be included in the analytics described below.

Purchases and restoration

Premium is an optional, one-time purchase. Apple or Google processes the payment; DMV Prep does not receive your full payment-card information. RevenueCat processes purchase history, transaction and store information, a pseudonymous app-user identifier and technical information to validate purchases, provide Premium access, prevent fraud and restore purchases. Purchase information also supports purchase reporting.

The app uses RevenueCat’s anonymous app-user identifiers. It does not ask you to create an account or provide your name or email address for a purchase. Apple and Google maintain their own purchase records.

Product analytics

DMV Prep uses Mixpanel to understand and improve how the app is used. Events may include screens viewed, selected study state and language, study-session counts and outcomes, purchase or restore outcomes, app version and OTA update number. Mixpanel also processes a pseudonymous app/device identifier and technical device information.

The app does not send your name, email address, question text or individual answers in analytics events. Automatic Mixpanel events and IP-based geolocation are disabled in the app. Network requests still reach the providers, which may process technical connection information. Selecting a study state is not a request for your device’s location.

Purpose and sharing

We use this information to operate Premium, restore access, respond to support requests and understand product usage. DMV Prep contains no advertising, and its implementation does not use these events to track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

Service providers process information for these purposes under their own applicable terms and privacy notices: RevenueCat, Mixpanel, Apple and Google. Mixpanel uses its default U.S. service endpoint in this app.

Retention, choices and requests

Local study progress can be reset for the selected state in Settings. Removing the app can remove local progress, but does not automatically remove purchase or analytics records held by service providers. Purchase records are used to support access and restoration over time; provider-held information is also subject to provider retention settings and applicable legal obligations.

You may contact us with questions or requests about access, correction or deletion. The information we can locate depends on the records and identifiers we can reliably associate with you; the app does not collect a name or email address with its analytics. Requests are subject to applicable law and purchase-record requirements. Please provide your platform, app version and relevant transaction reference if needed. Do not send passwords, full payment-card details or identity documents.

Support messages

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, including your email address. We use it to respond to your request.

Audience and external sites

DMV Prep is a driver-knowledge study aid, not a service directed to children under 13. Links to official handbooks and external websites open in the app’s browser; those sites have their own practices. These support pages have no analytics or third-party scripts. They are hosted by GitHub Pages, which processes hosting-related technical information under GitHub’s privacy statement.
