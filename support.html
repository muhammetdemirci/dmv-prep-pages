How can we help?

Help with studying, Premium and purchase restoration.

Contact us

Write to dev.demirci@gmail.com. Include your platform, app version and a short description of the issue. For a question correction, include the state, topic and question text.

Email support

Restore Premium

Open Premium in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account or Google Play account that made the purchase, and keep an internet connection available. Premium is a one-time, non-consumable purchase with no subscription or automatic renewal.

There is no DMV Prep account linking Apple and Google purchases, so automatic cross-platform access is not offered.

Free access and study progress

The free tier includes a fixed bank of 60 questions per state and one practice exam per state per local calendar day. Premium unlocks full banks for all 50 states, unlimited practice exams and full mistake review. Both tiers include English and Spanish.

Your study progress is stored on your device. Changing states keeps separate progress; Settings can reset the selected state. Deleting the app may remove local progress.

Refunds and billing

Apple handles App Store purchases and refund requests through Report a Problem. For Google Play purchases, use Google Play’s refund guidance. Store policies determine eligibility. Do not email passwords, payment-card details or identity documents.

Rules and sources

DMV Prep is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with a DMV or government agency. Questions are original practice materials, not official examination questions. Study banks combine shared driving fundamentals and selected local rules. Consult your state’s official handbook for current rules and test requirements. In the app, open Settings → Official sources & about.
