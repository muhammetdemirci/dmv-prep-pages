Delete your DMV Prep account and data

Account creation is optional. You can request deletion even if you cannot open the app.

In the app

  1. Open Settings → Premium account.
  2. Choose Delete DMV Prep account.
  3. Confirm your password and permanent deletion.

Without the app

Email dev.demirci@gmail.com from the address used for your DMV account, with the subject “DMV Prep account deletion”. Ask to delete your DMV account and related data. We will verify ownership before removing the records. Do not send your password or identity documents.

What is removed and retained

Deletion removes the DMV backend account and its RevenueCat customer connection. A Firebase sign-in used only by DMV is deleted. Existing Den application accounts and their data are preserved; a shared Firebase sign-in remains if Den uses it. Local study progress can be removed separately in Settings or by removing the app.

Account deletion does not refund a purchase. Apple and Google keep their store records; information needed for legal, security or transaction obligations may remain with providers. Anonymous analytics are not automatically linked to your email. For requests concerning a purchase or anonymous usage data, include the platform, app version and relevant transaction reference so we can establish what can be located. See the privacy policy.
