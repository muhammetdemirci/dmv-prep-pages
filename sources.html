Official information sources

DMV Prep is an independent study app. It does not represent a government entity and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or authorized by any DMV or government agency.

Original practice questions combine shared driving fundamentals with selected state rules. They are not official exam questions. This directory lists the handbook, statute, knowledge-test and road-safety references used by version 0.4.0 (Android 6).

The state sections list local references. Shared driving fundamentals also use the New York and California handbooks and the federal safety publications in the shared section. A shared reference is not a statement that every state has the same law. Always check your own state’s current handbook and requirements. Some study checks use app-defined formats; practice does not guarantee passing.

Links open the original publisher’s page or document. Arkansas DPS and Louisiana OMV publish some documents through media.ark.org and PowerDMS; both the government entry page and document are listed.

Where a cited address has moved or can be difficult to access, we provide a related government reference. The downloadable inventory records the exact citations used by this app version.

If a publisher’s website is unavailable from your location, use the USAGov state profile above its citations to find the state motor vehicle agency and government contacts.
